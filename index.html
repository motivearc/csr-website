Our Approach

Responsibility is not a programme we run. It is how we run the company.

Meridian Group designs, builds and maintains infrastructure that millions of people rely on every day — from water networks and transit systems to the buildings where they learn and recover. That reach brings an obligation: every decision we make has consequences well beyond our balance sheet.

Our responsibility strategy, Meridian 2030, sets clear, time-bound commitments across three pillars. Each is owned by a member of our Executive Committee, linked to leadership remuneration, and independently assured. Where we fall short, we say so — and explain what we are changing.

Our Pillars

Three commitments that guide every decision

Each pillar has its own targets, governance and public scorecard, reviewed quarterly by the Board Sustainability Committee.

Environment

Halving our operational emissions by 2030 and reaching net zero across our value chain by 2040, while restoring more nature than our projects disturb.

  • 100% renewable electricity by 2028
  • Biodiversity net gain on every new site
  • Zero waste to landfill from offices

Community

Investing 1% of pre-tax profit in the places we work, creating skilled jobs locally and giving every colleague three paid days a year to volunteer.

  • 5,000 apprenticeships by 2030
  • 40% local spend on major projects
  • Long-term partnerships, not one-off grants

Ethics & Governance

Holding ourselves to the highest standards of integrity, transparency and human rights — throughout our own operations and our 9,000-strong supply chain.

  • Independent Speak Up line in 28 languages
  • Human rights due diligence on all tier-1 suppliers
  • 40% women in senior leadership by 2030

Impact in 2025

Progress you can measure

Figures for the 2025 financial year, independently assured by Halden & Pryce LLP to ISAE 3000 limited assurance.

  • 184,000 tonnes CO₂e avoided since our 2019 baseline
  • 78% of electricity from renewable sources
  • 96,400 employee volunteering hours in 2025
  • 312 communities supported across 34 countries
  • 1,860 apprentices trained through Pathways to Trade
  • £12.4m invested in community programmes

Featured Initiatives

Programmes making a difference on the ground

Environment

Living Corridors

Restoring 2,400 hectares of native habitat alongside our rail and road projects, reconnecting wildlife routes fragmented by decades of development.

Read more about Living Corridors

Working with ecologists from the Northfield Wildlife Trust, every project now begins with a baseline biodiversity survey. Since 2022 we have planted 1.1 million native trees and hedgerow plants, created 46 new wetland ponds and recorded returning populations of great crested newts and dormice at monitored sites.

Community

Pathways to Trade

A paid, 18-month apprenticeship programme for young people not in education or employment, with a guaranteed job interview on completion.

Read more about Pathways to Trade

Run with further-education colleges in 14 cities, Pathways combines on-site training in electrical, civil and digital engineering with mentoring from Meridian colleagues. 1,860 apprentices have completed the programme to date, and 83% are still in skilled employment a year later.

Ethics & Governance

Fair Supply Charter

A shared standard for fair pay, safe conditions and prompt payment, co-designed with our suppliers and audited by independent assessors.

Read more about the Fair Supply Charter

All 1,240 strategic suppliers have now signed the Charter. We pay small businesses within 21 days on average, and in 2025 our audits identified and resolved 37 labour-practice issues — each one published, anonymised, in our Modern Slavery Statement.
